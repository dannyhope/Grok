# Split paragraphs in the restructured panel
**Refined:** 2026-02-08
**Done when:** Users can click within a transclusion paragraph in the restructured panel, choose "Split", and it becomes two separate transclusions — both maintaining their relationship to the original source paragraph.

## Details
- User clicks at a position within a transclusion paragraph in the right panel
- A "Split" option appears (e.g. context menu or inline button)
- The paragraph splits into two transclusions at the cursor position
- Both resulting transclusions reference the original source paragraph (via character offsets)
- Source panel (left) is unaffected — the original paragraph remains whole
